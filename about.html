About

One person. That’s the whole pitch.

N

I’m Nick. I build websites and automation for small businesses around Northampton, and I’m the only person here.

That’s usually the first question people have, so I’ll answer it up front: yes, it’s just me. No team, no office, no account manager who forwards your email to someone you’ve never met.

The reason that works in your favor is math. A real agency has payroll and a sales team, so their smallest project has to start somewhere north of five figures. A $900 website isn’t a small job to them — it’s a job they lose money on, so they say no, politely, and you go back to having no website. I don’t have that floor. I can take the $900 job, do it properly, and still make a living.

The trade-off is honest and you should know it: I’m one person, so I take a handful of projects at a time, and I’ll tell you if I can’t start for three weeks. I won’t pretend to be bigger than I am to win the work.

I work with auto shops, electricians, roofers, landscapers, and the kind of small service businesses that keep the Valley running. Most of them are excellent at what they do and have a website that suggests otherwise. That gap is the whole job.

I live in Northampton. If you’re within about twenty minutes of me I’ll come to you, look at how you actually work, and tell you honestly whether a website is even the thing you need. Sometimes it isn’t — sometimes it’s just your Google listing and a booking link, and that’s a much cheaper conversation.
