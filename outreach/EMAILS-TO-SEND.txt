What actually worked for this list: Google Maps for a trade plus a
town, then open each result's website on your phone. Rank what you
find:

  BEST   "Account Suspended" / "This site can't be reached" /
         "Coming Soon" that's been up for years. A working business
         with a dead or unbuilt site. Two of the ten above.

  GOOD   No website at all - just a Google listing or Facebook page.
         Highest value, but you'll have to call or walk in; there's
         usually no email address published.

  GOOD   Text you can't pinch to zoom, or a page that loads at
         desktop width and makes you scroll sideways.

  OK     A copyright year three or more years old in the footer.

  OK     No way to book or request a quote without calling. Most
         common gap by far, and the easiest sale because you're
         adding something rather than criticising something.

Write down the specific thing and the date you checked it. One true
specific observation beats three paragraphs about your services.

Nobody enjoys being told their website is ugly. Being told it's
BROKEN, by a neighbour, with the fix included - that's a completely
different conversation, and it's the only reason any of these emails
have a chance.
