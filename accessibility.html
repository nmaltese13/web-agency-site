What I will not claim

No fake “ADA certified” promise.

Automated testing can only verify part of WCAG. I will not sell an overlay, call a scanner result a complete audit, or tell you a site is legally compliant based only on automated checks.

If your engagement requires formal certification, a signed VPAT/ACR, or specialist assistive-technology testing beyond the agreed scope, I will say that before the work starts rather than pretend.
